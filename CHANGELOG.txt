FIRA v1.0 - Smart Map Search
- Search ODP names and attributes (STO, desa, kecamatan, kabupaten, branch, cluster, city).
- Search public map locations such as roads, housing estates and landmarks via bounded Nominatim results, filtered against the official Kabupaten Bekasi polygon.
- Selecting a map location moves the map only; it does not change the radius center.
- Selecting an ODP opens its profile.
